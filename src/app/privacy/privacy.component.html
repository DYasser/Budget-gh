Privacy

budget.io has no server, no account and no analytics. Everything you enter stays in your own browser, on the device you entered it on.

cloud_off What we collect

Nothing.

There is no backend to send data to. No account to create, no sign-in, no email address. Your budget is never transmitted anywhere, which also means nobody — including whoever built this — can read it.

Because this lives in your browser rather than on a server, two things follow. Clearing your site data deletes your budget permanently, and your budget does not follow you to another browser or device. Use Settings → Export to keep a copy as a JSON file.

public Requests that leave your device

One, and it carries none of your data: the app loads the Material Symbols icon font from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Like any request for a font, that tells Google your IP address and browser — not anything about your budget.

After your first visit the font is cached by the app's service worker, so it loads from your device on later visits. The site itself is served as static files from GitHub Pages, whose own logging is described in GitHub's privacy statement.

block What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting
  • No advertising or tracking pixels
  • No cookies
  • No third-party scripts beyond the icon font above
  • No connection to any bank or financial institution

delete Deleting your data

Delete individual categories from the Expenses and Incomes pages, or clear everything at once through your browser's “clear site data” setting for this site. There is nothing held elsewhere that needs a request to erase.

code Verifying this

Every claim here is checkable: the app is open source, so you can read the code that stores your data and confirm there is nowhere for it to be sent.
